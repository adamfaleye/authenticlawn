Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know before getting started with Authentic Lawn Care.

How long have you been in business?

Authentic Lawn Care & Landscaping has been proudly serving the Winston-Salem area for over 25 years. We've built our reputation on quality work, honest pricing, and showing up when we say we will.

Do you provide free estimates?

Absolutely. Fill out the quote form above or give us a call. We'll assess your property and give you a detailed, no-obligation estimate — usually within 24 hours.

Do you service commercial properties?

Yes! We provide full-service lawn care and landscaping for offices, retail spaces, HOAs, and other commercial properties throughout the Triad area.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Winston-Salem, Clemmons, Lewisville, Kernersville, High Point, and surrounding communities. Not sure if you're in our area? Just ask — we're happy to check.

How quickly can you start?

In most cases, we can begin within a week of your initial consultation. For seasonal services like spring cleanup and aeration, we recommend booking early as slots fill fast.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Your satisfaction is guaranteed. If something isn't right, let us know and we'll come back to fix it at no extra charge. That's the Authentic promise.
